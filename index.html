Travel journal

Three days in Paris on a budget

Published 12 September 2026

Getting around

The easiest way to explore Paris is the metro. A weekly Navigo pass covers all zones and costs less than buying single tickets. Walking is also a great option, because many famous sights are close to each other along the Seine.

Where to see the best views

Everyone wants to visit the Eiffel Tower, but the view from Montmartre is completely free. Go at sunset and you will see the whole city light up.

Island hopping in Greece

Published 3 August 2026

Choosing your islands

Greece has more than 200 inhabited islands, so planning is important. For a first trip, combine Santorini for views, Naxos for beaches and Mykonos for nightlife.

Ferries and timing

Ferries are cheaper than flights and much more scenic. Book early in July and August, when boats sell out quickly. You can read more about the islands on the Cyclades page.

First time in Tokyo: what to know

Published 20 June 2026

Etiquette basics

In Japan, tipping is not expected and can even feel rude. Speak quietly on trains, and always take off your shoes when entering a home or a traditional ryokan.

Saving money with a rail pass

If you plan to visit other cities, the JR Pass can save you a lot. Check the current prices on the Japan Rail Pass page before you buy.

New York in winter

Published 15 January 2026

Free things to do

New York can be expensive, but many of the best experiences cost nothing. Walk across the Brooklyn Bridge, take the free Staten Island Ferry for a view of the Statue of Liberty, or explore Central Park covered in snow.

Staying warm

Temperatures in January often drop below −5 °C, and the wind makes it feel even colder. Pack a warm coat, gloves and a hat, and plan indoor stops like museums between long walks.

Traveling tips

Before your trip

  1. Check that your passport is valid for at least 6 months after your return date.
  2. Find out whether you need a visa for your destination.
  3. Buy travel insurance that covers medical costs.
  4. Make copies of your documents and save them online.
  5. Tell your bank which countries you will visit.

What to pack

  • Passport or ID card
  • Phone charger and a travel adapter
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Basic medicine and a small first aid kit
  • A reusable water bottle
